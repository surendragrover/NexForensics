🔎 Deep OSINT (DOA)

Built for investigative journalists: check a person or company before you publish, and get a source-backed dossier your editor and legal team can verify.

  • Background screening: sanctions lists (UK, OFAC, UN, EU, World Bank), SEBI enforcement orders, ICIJ Offshore Leaks, SEC EDGAR filings and dark-web leak-site monitoring, in one run.
  • Your own documents: upload PDFs, Word files, web pages and scanned images (OCR, English and Hindi).
  • Contradiction finder: flags where a public statement conflicts with records, such as a denied loan or a disputed directorship, and builds a timeline.
  • Right person, not a namesake: uses date of birth, city and organisations to separate the subject from people with the same name.
  • Face comparison (optional): compares a subject photo with the faces found in your documents.
  • Ask your own questions, one per line, and get each answered against the evidence.
  • Court-ready PDF: every finding cites its source and how many independent sources back it, with a SHA-256 fingerprint and a checklist of what was and was not searched.
